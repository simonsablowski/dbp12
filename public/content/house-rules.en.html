1. Scope

These house rules and terms apply to short stays in the flat at Dora-Benjamin-Park 12, 10245 Berlin. By booking, you confirm that you have read them and that you and everyone travelling with you will follow them.

2. Arrival, departure and keys

  • Check-in is possible between 15:00 and 20:00. Please agree other times with us in advance.
  • Check-out is by 11:00.
  • We hand over the key in person. Please tell us shortly before your arrival roughly when you will arrive.
  • If a key is lost, you pay for the replacement and, if necessary for security reasons, for replacing the lock.

3. Occupancy

The flat is suitable for a maximum of three adults and three children. Only the people named in the booking may stay overnight. Visitors during the day are fine. Parties and events are not allowed.

4. Smoking and pets

Smoking is not allowed in the flat, in the stairwell or out of the windows. This also applies to e-cigarettes. Pets are not allowed.

5. Consideration for neighbours

  • Please be considerate of the neighbours. Quiet hours apply between 22:00 and 6:00 and all day on Sundays and public holidays: no loud music and no loud conversations in the stairwell, in the courtyard or with the windows open.
  • Please close the doors in the stairwell quietly.

6. Waste

  • Please separate your waste into general waste, packaging (yellow bin), paper and organic waste. We will show you where the bins are at key handover.
  • Glass goes into the public glass containers, only on working days between 7:00 and 20:00, never on Sundays or public holidays.
  • Please take all waste downstairs before you leave.

7. Bathroom and shower

The shower is level with the floor, without a tray or edge. The water drains through a channel with a strainer that quickly becomes blocked by hair. When this happens, the water does not drain fast enough, spreads across the bathroom and can reach the hallway. Please therefore:

  • Check that the strainer is clear before you shower, and remove hair after showering.
  • Watch whether the water is draining while you shower. If it starts to collect, turn off the water and clean the strainer.
  • Wipe up any water on the floor immediately. Standing water can damage the floor and the flat below.
  • Do not put wet wipes, hygiene products or food waste into the toilet or the drain.

8. Care and liability

  • Please treat the flat and its furnishings with care, as you would treat your own home.
  • You are liable for damage that you, the people travelling with you or your visitors cause through fault, for example through improper use, through escaped water or by not following these house rules. Normal wear and tear is not affected.
  • In case of damage, you pay the costs of repair, cleaning or replacement at current value. In many cases, a personal liability insurance covers such damage.
  • If the flat is dirtier than usual (for example from smoking), we charge for the additional cleaning.
  • Please report damage, defects or escaped water to us immediately, even if you did not cause it. The earlier we know, the smaller the damage.
  • We are not liable for valuables you bring with you.

9. Energy and safety

  • When you leave the flat, please close the windows, switch off the stove and the lights, and lock the door.
  • Please heat and ventilate economically: open the windows fully for a few minutes several times a day rather than leaving them tilted.

10. Payment

The total price (nights and cleaning fee) is payable in cash on arrival at key handover, unless you chose another payment method when booking. You will receive a receipt.

11. Cancellation

The booking is binding. If you cannot come, please cancel by email as early as possible so that we can offer the flat to someone else.

For bookings paid online (once available): you can cancel free of charge up to 7 days before arrival. If you cancel later or do not arrive, we charge 50% of the accommodation price, unless we can rent the flat to someone else for that period.

12. House rights

In case of a serious breach of these house rules, for example a party, more guests than booked or significant disturbance of the neighbours, we may end the stay early. The price for nights already booked will not be refunded in that case.
